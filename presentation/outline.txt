Qupacabrathon 2026 — presentation plan

Communication job
By the end, judges should understand why our C_21 one-shot plan is a deliberate,
budget-constrained trade—and that it has 67% modeled certification power at the
declared deficit rather than a 90% guarantee.

Audience
Qupacabrathon judges, mentors, and technically curious teams.

Purpose
Compete for Best Explanation while making the experiment-design choices easy to
evaluate. This is a pre-hardware deck; it does not claim a hardware result.

Narrative arc (9 slides / approximately 5 minutes)

1. C_21 run plan
   Introduce the proposed Emerald experiment and the pre-hardware status.

2. Odd-cycle correlations create a measurable quantum advantage
   Explain the odd-cycle game, the classical ceiling, and why a statistically
   certified quantum advantage matters more than a simulated win rate.

3. The same local circuit structure covers all 42 questions
   Show the Bell-pair preparation and local rotations. Emphasize locality and
   the identical circuit structure across all 2n questions.

4. The declared device deficit determines the feasible cycle size
   Define device deficit delta and use the affordable-frontier chart to show
   how it reduces feasible cycle size while raising the shot requirement.

5. C_21 fits the cap, but the 90% sizing target does not
   Contrast the submitted 110 shots per circuit with the 165 required for 90%
   certification power at δ = 0.0150. Make the budget/power trade explicit.

6. C_21 fits the budget with a meaningful certification risk
   Present the complete run plan: 42 circuits, 110 shots each, 4,620 shots,
   $19.99, 67% certification power, and a $0.01 budget remainder.

7. The award objectives favor different run designs
   Compare the submitted C_21 plan with the unaffordable 90%-power C_21 sizing
   target and identify the deliberate risk choice.

8. Emerald maximizes power under the cap, even though C_21 remains below 90%
   Use the QPU power chart to show why Emerald is selected, then summarize the
   single-run, textbook-angle, and one-variant choices with the caveat that the
   C_21 shot count is below the 90% sizing target.

9. The hardware result will test the noise estimate
   Close on the measurable standard: expected 0.983602 win rate versus the
   0.976190 classical bound, certified at 3 sigma. State the post-run analysis
   path and the honest current status.

Visual direction
Deep ink background, electric-lime decision accents, cyan quantum accents, and
warm coral risk accents. Large typography, a single focal composition per slide,
minimal interface chrome, and CSS-built lines/plots rather than external assets.
