Qupacabrathon 2026 — presentation plan

Communication job
By the end, judges should understand why our C_11 one-shot plan is a deliberate,
evidence-backed trade under the $20 cap—and exactly what the hardware run must
demonstrate to certify the result.

Audience
Qupacabrathon judges, mentors, and technically curious teams.

Purpose
Compete for Best Explanation while making the experiment-design choices easy to
evaluate. This is a pre-hardware deck; it does not claim a hardware result.

Narrative arc (9 slides / approximately 5 minutes)

1. C_11 run plan
   Introduce the proposed Emerald experiment and the pre-hardware status.

2. Odd-cycle correlations create a measurable quantum advantage
   Explain the odd-cycle game, the classical ceiling, and why a statistically
   certified quantum advantage matters more than a simulated win rate.

3. The same local circuit structure covers all 22 questions
   Show the Bell-pair preparation and local rotations. Emphasize locality and
   the identical circuit structure across all 2n questions.

4. The declared device deficit determines the feasible cycle size
   Define the device deficit delta and show how it consumes the quantum-to-
   classical margin. Make clear that delta is our declared assumption.

5. Asymmetric readout moved the viable target from C_13 to C_11
   Contrast the earlier optimistic delta assumptions with asymmetric Emerald
   readout. Show both current scenarios and explain that the corrected search
   rejected C_13 rather than labeling a low-power candidate a winner.

6. C_11 meets the power and budget criteria
   Present the complete run plan: 22 circuits, 380 shots each, 8,360 shots,
   $19.976, 91% certification power, and 0.0001 deficit headroom.

7. The award objectives favor different run designs
   Compare the C_11 largest-cycle bet with the lower-cost C_9 design-efficiency
   alternative and the shot-heavy C_5 nonlocal-content chase.

8. Plan A prioritizes certification power over optional features
   Explain the strategic choices: no paid calibration run, no twirl task fees,
   textbook-optimal angles, and Emerald over Garnet for the lower observed
   readout error despite its higher per-shot price.

9. The hardware result will test the noise estimate
   Close on the measurable standard: expected 0.964011 win rate versus the
   0.954545 classical bound, certified at 3 sigma. State the post-run analysis
   path and the honest current status.

Visual direction
Deep ink background, electric-lime decision accents, cyan quantum accents, and
warm coral risk accents. Large typography, a single focal composition per slide,
minimal interface chrome, and CSS-built lines/plots rather than external assets.
