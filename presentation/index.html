Circuit strategy

One shared four-gate structure — H, CNOT, then local rotations Ry(Ax), Ry(By) — answers all 42 questions; only the local angles change per question.

03

The deficit

δ = ωq − ωhardware: device noise eats into the quantum advantage available for statistical certification. Higher δ lowers the largest affordable cycle and raises the required shots per circuit.

04

Model discipline

At the declared δ = 0.015, 110 submitted shots deliver 67% certification power. Reaching a 90% target needs 165 shots/circuit — $23.69, over the $20 cap.

33% of repetitions fail certification at 110 shots, even if δ is accurate

Plan A — selected run

Award strategy

07

Tradeoffs

08

Chirality test

Inconclusive, not negative. Without calibrated, uniform per-qubit error rates, mapping-dependent noise can't be separated from a genuine chirality signal on today's hardware.
