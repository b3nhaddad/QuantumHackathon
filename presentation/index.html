Design choices

Plan A prioritizes certification power over optional features.

01
Single-run allocation

No paid calibration run; the available budget is assigned to the C11 attempt.

02
Textbook-optimal angles

No perturbation outperformed the simple local strategy with defensible power.

03
No paid twirl variants

The model did not provide evidence that extra task fees would improve certification power.

04
Emerald selected

Its lower observed readout error outweighed the slightly higher per-shot price.
